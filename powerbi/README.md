# Sales Analysis using Power BI

## 1. Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of sales performance, profit performance, customer activity, product performance, payment methods, regions, categories, and monthly trends.

The project is designed to help users understand business performance and identify important sales and profit patterns through interactive visualizations.

---

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Monitor total sales and total orders.
* Analyze total customers.
* Track profit margin.
* Compare sales across different categories.
* Analyze sales by payment mode.
* Analyze sales across different regions.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Identify low-performing products.
* Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
* Compare sales and profit performance.
* Provide an interactive business dashboard using Power BI.

## 4. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is titled:

**Sales & Profit Performance Overview**

It provides a consolidated view of important sales and profit metrics.

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes:

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Orders**
* **Total Customers**
* **Profit Margin**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall business performance.

---

## 5. Sales Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales using multiple business dimensions.

### Sales by Category

This visualization compares sales across different product categories.

It helps identify categories contributing the most to overall sales.

### Sales by Payment Mode

Sales are analyzed according to different payment methods.

This helps understand customer payment preferences and their contribution to sales.

### Sales by Region

The dashboard provides regional sales analysis.

This helps identify regions with stronger sales performance.

---

## 6. Monthly Sales and Profit Trend

The dashboard includes a monthly trend analysis comparing:

* Sales
* Profit

This visualization helps identify changes in business performance over time.

It can be used to observe:

* Monthly sales growth
* Monthly profit changes
* High-performing periods
* Low-performing periods
* Differences between sales and profit trends

## 7. Product Performance Analysis

The dashboard analyzes individual product performance.

### Top 10 Products

The Top 10 Products visualization identifies products generating the highest sales.

This helps businesses understand their strongest-performing products.

### Bottom 10 Products

The Bottom 10 Products visualization identifies products with comparatively lower sales performance.

This can help businesses investigate products that may require pricing, marketing, inventory, or sales strategy changes.

## 8. Sales vs Profit Performance

The dashboard also provides a comparison between:

* Sales

This helps understand whether higher sales are translating into higher profitability.

The comparison can support business decisions related to:

* Product performance
