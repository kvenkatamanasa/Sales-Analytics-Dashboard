Project Overview

Turning raw sales data into actionable business insights.

Business Problem

Businesses need to understand sales performance, product demand, customer behavior, regional trends and profitability to make better decisions.

Solution

The project cleans and analyzes sales data using SQL and Excel and presents meaningful insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Outcome

The dashboard provides a clear view of sales, profit, products, customers and regional performance to support data-driven decision making.
